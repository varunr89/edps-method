> The foundational text of modern economics. Examines how nations become wealthy through division of labor, free markets, and limited government.

---

## Core Claims

1. **division_of_labor**: Division of labor is the primary driver of productivity and wealth
2. **market_extent**: The extent of the market limits the degree of specialization possible
3. **price_formation**: Natural price emerges from competition; market price fluctuates around it
4. **wages_profits_rents**: Output is distributed among wages (labor), profits (capital), and rents (land)
5. **capital_accumulation**: Savings become investment; capital accumulation drives growth
6. **free_trade**: Trade restrictions harm wealth; nations benefit from specialization and exchange
7. **limited_government**: Government should provide defense, justice, and public works—little else
