### 4. Define: Propensity to truck, barter, and exchange
What does Smith mean by the "propensity to truck, barter, and exchange," and why is it significant?

**Answer:** Propensity to truck, barter, and exchange is the innate human desire to exchange goods for self-interest. This is significant because it leads to division of labor (more goods to exchange) and creation of an economy resulting in sustained progress.


---

### 5. Define: Common stock
What does Smith mean when he says that exchange brings different talents into a "common stock"?

**Answer:** Common stock refers to good or services that have economic or exchange value. Beer, bread, meat are all example of common stock which encourages people to specialize as brewer, baker, and butcher respectively. Each occupation is able to produce excess which helps others have access to these goods. An example of non-common stock is say a farmer who specializes in farming weeds - it takes talent but is of no value to anyone else.

### 7. Counterfactual
According to Smith, what would society look like if humans lacked the disposition to truck, barter, and exchange? How would this affect differences in talent between people?

**Answer:** If humans lacked the disposition to truck, barter, and exchange- there would be no division of labor as each individual would have perform all work needed for his sustenance. There would be no economic progress and we would be worse than the cave man (even the cave men bartered/exchanged). If there is no division of labor, there would minimal differences in talents of people as they would have no need or the time to apply themselves fully to the talent.
