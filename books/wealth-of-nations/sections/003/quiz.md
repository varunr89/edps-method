### 6. Type: Comparative
How does Smith’s “extent of the market” compare to modern digital platforms (e.g., app stores, marketplaces) in enabling specialization?

**Length:** 2-3 sentences

**Your Answer:**

**Model Answer:**
Both enlarge accessible demand and lower transaction costs, letting niche producers find sufficient buyers. Platforms act like “new waterways,” expanding reach instantly and supporting finer specialization. When platforms or logistics are disrupted, specialization contracts, mirroring transport shocks.
