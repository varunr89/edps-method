### 6. Teach It Back

Explain to a smart 15-year-old why Smith believes agriculture cannot benefit from the division of labour as much as manufacturing can.

**Answer:** The farmer can only do specific tasks at specific times of the year, which prevents him from specializing. For example, in spring the farmer focuses on plowing and tilling his field. Then in early summer he has to focus on planting the seeds. Then he focuses on maintaining his farm while the plants grow. And finally, he focuses during harvest season on harvesting. Each of these are distinct tasks, and the farmer is forced to do them at specific times of the year and is unable to specialize in any one. Anything dependent on nature enforces a seasonality that prevents division of labor from being applied as it can be in manufacturing.

---

### 7. Counterfactual

According to Smith's logic, what would happen to innovation and machinery invention if workers were not specialized but instead performed many different tasks throughout the day?

**Answer:** Innovation and machinery will decline if a person is not specialized. This will happen because a person focusing on many tasks cannot find opportunities for efficiency gains; his attention is spread across tasks preventing him from noticing the minute details, which are critical for innovation and machinery invention.
